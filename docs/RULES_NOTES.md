# Arkham Horror (Chaosium 1987) — rules digest

Transcribed from `ENG/02 rules.pdf`, `ENG/03 about your investigator.pdf`, `ENG/01 …errata…pdf`.
This is the engine's reference; every engine rule should trace back to a line here.

## Setup
1. Decks: Gates, Items, Spells on board. Local Characters (2), Retainers (4), Skills, Charity aside.
   Monsters (49 incl. 5 Powers) into the cup; Doom Factor counter reserved.
2. Players choose investigators. 1 player → 3 investigators; 2–3 players → 2 each.
3. (Table-talk only.)
4. Each investigator: $13, 3 Item cards (only 2 if >4 investigators), 1 Skill, 1 Spell, 1 Charity.
   Trading cards/money allowed any time when in the same space/location; skills not tradeable once play begins.
5. Split 10 points between Strength and Sanity, each 3..7.
6. All investigators start at the Train Station.
7. Top Gate card; roll Gate Appearance Table → location; place gate, 3 random monsters face up there.
   Doom Factor on space 1.
8. Each player rolls 2D6; highest goes first, then play to the left.

## Order of play
Investigator Phase (each investigator in turn):
- Collect $ if on Retainer; return to Arkham if starting in Other World box 1.
1. Acts: wait / move / move and take taxi.
2. Encounters: wait / roll encounter / +SAN / +STR / money / purchase-find item / use skill / use charity / meet monster.
3. Meets monster: sanity roll, (maybe) temporarily insane, wait / sneak / fight.
4. Fights: attacks, risks counterattack.

Mythos Phase:
1. Random gate appears (roll Gate Appearance Table).
2. All monsters move.
3. All monsters attack (sanity rolls, monster attacks, investigator sneaks or counterattacks).

## Winning and losing
- Win: in game turn 2+, all gates eliminated.
- Lose (a): N gates present for a full game turn: 1–3 investigators → 8; (3–)4 → 7; 5+ → 6.
- Lose (b): Doom track counts every gate that appears (incl. destroyed or donated to Dagon). Reaching
  "Doom of Arkham" (past 13) loses.
- First Citizen of Arkham: surviving investigator who closed most gates; tie → most monster trophies.

## Investigator Step 1: Acts
- Move: roll 2D6, move up to that many spaces (fewer or none). Entering/exiting a location = 1 space.
  Entering/exiting a gate costs no movement.
- Entering a location stops movement (may still end turn in an Other World).
- Entering a space/location with a monster → must stop, except:
  (1) vehicle ride moves through most monsters; if no alternate route, a <blocker> stops vehicle one space distant from it;
  (2) investigator with Mists of R'lyeh; (3) may move through Hound of Tindalos unless the Hound is in a building.
- Pointers show the street spaces from which a location is entered. No exact count needed.
- Upon entering a location: meet any monster(s) there, or roll D6 on the location table. If gated,
  may instead transit via gate to the Other World (avoid location event). If a gate appears due to a
  location event, investigator is swept to the Other World involuntarily (no monster meeting, no sanity loss).
- Entering an Other World: immediately roll on its gate table. Transition costs no movement.
- A location event may send investigator to another location: roll again on that table (unless monsters there → meet them).
- Gated location: choose gate table (token → box 2) or location table (stays in Arkham).
- Monsters never pursue through gates.
- Monster + gate present: may Sneak past monster into Other World after surviving sanity roll; failed Sneak →
  1 die of damage to Strength; if survives, may Sneak again.
- Other World: box 2 roll on gate table; next turn move to box 1 and roll again; third turn return to Arkham
  at the gate of departure. Follow the order of play normally while there.
- 0 Sanity or 0 Strength in an Other World → investigator lost: return/reshuffle cards, trophies to monster pile;
  start another investigator.
  OPTION: an investigator lost in an Other World can be rescued if another investigator lands on the same box.
  Two ways: the lost investigator can be shifted from box to box and back to Arkham with a successful strength roll
  by the rescuer for each move; or the rescuer can give the lost investigator sanity or strength points by a spell or
  magical item. The lost investigator's player must do nothing during the turns needed, or the players agree that
  one player can have more than one investigator in play.
- Returning: begin movement in matching Arkham gate location (choose if two gates to same world). If a monster
  guards the gate, may move away without sanity roll. To attack the gate, wait for attack step, taking sanity
  loss for guardian. If gate destroyed, guardians are sucked away and added to investigator's trophies.
- Taxi: on a Taxi space (or Taxi Whistle) pay $1 → go to any space/location in Arkham, concluding movement.
  Free rides from location events: treat as free taxi.

## Investigator Step 2: Encounters
- Empty street spaces: no effect.
- A free ride is declinable unless the Sheldon Gang gives it. Results may be optional.
- Gate+monster from a location result: draw one of each, place; investigator swept to the Other World without
  meeting the monster; take result from that gate table.
- Gated location: enter gate or roll location table. A monster in a location is an encounter in itself (no roll).
  A successful Sneak may take the investigator to a different encounter.
- Only one gate per location. A second gate appearance there adds only the monster.

## Investigator Step 3: Meeting Monsters
- SAN a/b: lose a with successful D6 sanity roll, b on failure. 0/0 → not mind-threatening.
- Only one sanity roll per individual monster per game turn.
- Sneak away from more than one species in a space: roll Sneak first, then one sanity roll for the single most
  ghastly type. Only roll sanity if Sneak succeeds; if Sneak fails you make the sanity roll before fighting.
- Attacking: sanity roll for each monster attacked, just before the individual attack.
- Sanity 0 → Sanitarium immediately (no movement cost, out of phase). Discard one spell of choice as penalty.
  Going insane mid-combat protects the investigator from monster attention.
- Sneak: roll D6 ≤ Sneak skill → move to an adjacent space or location of player's choice. If monsters occupy all
  adjacent spaces, no Sneak. Hound of Tindalos cannot be escaped by Sneaking. May Sneak past a gate guardian into
  the Other World. In an Other World, successful Sneak removes the monster, investigator stays in the box.

## Investigator Step 4: Fights
- Attack = Fight skill + one or two hand items (two 1H or one 2H) + other magical items + spells.
- Fight skill can always be added to any combination of physical weapons; Fight doesn't affect monsters immune to
  physical attack.
- Total = spells actually cast + items actually used + Fight skill + D6. Monster may be immune to all/some
  physical attacks, but the D6 always counts.
- Total ≥ monster SP → monster is killed → trophy stack.
- Otherwise investigator suffers automatic loss of D6 strength (counterattack).
- Strength 0 in Arkham → Hospital immediately, lose one Item of choice. Strength 0 in Other World → new investigator.
- May attack as many times as there are targets in the space. A monster may defend any number of times.
- Several monsters: choose order; sanity roll before each attack; ignore sanity for a 2nd monster of the same species.
- If both survive a round, fight again if the investigator wishes, or Sneak.

## Destroying Gates
- Any combination of magical and physical attacks + D6, during step 4. If not destroyed, wait until next turn.
- Must first find both sides: go through the gate and return. Returning to Arkham end, attack ≥ gate SP destroys it.
  Leaving the gated location before destroying → must rediscover (except Dragon's Eye / Blue Watcher holders).
- Guardians of a destroyed gate are sucked away → added to investigator's trophies.
- Add the gate to the trophy stack.
- Colour purge: monsters in play colour-keyed to a just-closed gate's Other World go back to the monster pile
  (not into trophies).
- Bind Monster spell (and physical/magic combos) can destroy a gate (after finding both sides).
- Dragon's Eye / Blue Watcher: take to a gate; no need to find both sides. After any encounter at the location the
  gate closes and the card goes back into the Items deck. Subtract personal damage (D6 STR).
- Elder Sign: after finding both sides, place on gate + spend 2 sanity → gate destroyed. Cannot be moved. Blocks
  future gate and monster appearances at that location (Hounds of Tindalos may still enter/attack there).
  Card: keep gate as trophy; move Doom Factor back by 1.

## Mythos Step 1: Gates Appear
- Gates appear (1) as a location event, (2) in Random Gate Appears step.
- Roll Gate Appearance table. If a gate appears draw a gate card + random monster, place them. If the location
  already has a gate, place only the monster. Elder Sign location → nothing.
- Investigator suffering "Gate and Monster Appear" is sucked through the gate to the Other World; monster stays on
  the location, to move and attack in Arkham.
- Gate card face-up once an investigator goes through (destination known); otherwise face down.

## Monsters Appear
- From Gate Appearance step, location event, or gate event. Draw random counter, place where the investigator is.
- Monster appears in an Other World: draw from pile. Investigator wins → trophy. Monster wins → D6 STR damage.
  If investigator perishes, monster back to pile.

## Monster pieces
- Front: silhouette, arrow if it moves, circle/diamond if handed, class (<blocker>, <flyer>, <power>, <vampire>),
  handedness letter + speed (L-5, R-4, F-6 flyer, 0 = stationary).
- Back: species, class, handedness+speed, SP, SAN a/b, special notation.
- Head the arrow in its direction of movement.
- Colour-matched monsters return to the pile when the gate of that colour is destroyed (not trophies; may return to play).
- After moving silhouette-up, flip to attack/be attacked; resolve; flip back. A surviving monster may be positioned
  in the space as the player chooses (i.e. arrow direction may be re-chosen).
- Diamond = intersection repeater (see About Your Investigator terms).

## Mythos Step 2: Monsters Move
- Speed printed on piece. Monsters attack by moving into spaces with investigators.
- Monsters appear in locations and move out of locations, but cannot move into locations (Tindalos exception).
- Monsters patrol ceaselessly; stop after max movement or when meeting an investigator in a space.
- Direction: placed on location; arrow determines direction. At intersections move L or R per handedness.
- Flyers: no handedness; F-n; move on streets always toward the nearest investigator. Equidistant → attack the one with
  highest Fight; equal Fights → coin flip.

## Mythos Step 3: Monsters Attack
- Monster attacks by moving into space/location with investigator(s). Determine sanity loss.
- Nearest monster moving into the space attacks the investigator with the higher Fight skill. Ties → coin flip;
  the higher monster attacks the higher investigator etc. Then sanity rolls.
- Investigator total = D6 + Fight + applicable spells and items vs monster SP. ≥ → investigator wins, trophy.
  Lower → lose D6 STR.
- Monster continues to attack until target no longer occupies the space, location, or Other World.
- Alone of monsters, Tindalos goes back into the pile after any attack which costs an investigator strength points.
- Bind Monster: a monster may be directed to attack another monster.

# About Your Investigator

## Investigators
- STR + SAN = 10, each column ≥ 3 (max 7). Not interchangeable afterwards.
- New investigator mid-game: begins play next turn in regular order.

## Strength
- Points earned above 7 are lost. Strength rolls: D6 ≤ current strength succeeds.
- 0 STR in Arkham → Hospital immediately, lose one item (choice) if any.
- 0 STR in Other World → lost; return cards to stacks, shuffle, new investigator.
- OPTION: carrying limit = strength (one item per point); can't pick up more unless STR exceeds items carried or drop something.

## Sanity
- Above 7 lost. Sanity roll D6 ≤ current sanity → lose first number, else second.
- Multiple species in a space/location: sanity roll for each as combated. Sneaking from a monster-occupied
  space: roll immediately only for the monster with greatest potential impact against sanity.
  Carried by nightgaunt through monster-infested location → no sanity roll.
- Cannot exhaust sanity to cast a spell (can't pay sanity cost that would bring SAN to 0? → "cannot exhaust sanity in order to cast a spell").
- 0 SAN → temporarily insane → Sanitarium; lose 1 spell card of choice if any.
- Insane in Other World → lost.
- Heinously attacking another investigator or destroying a gate thereby stranding an investigator → D6 sanity loss from remorse.

## Skills
- Skill roll: D6 ≤ skill value succeeds. Skill cards augment (+1 each, stack).

## Spells
- Each spell card = one usable spell (book name, spell, sanity cost, attack strength/function).
- Can only use spells held; unlimited number held; each spell card usable once per game turn.
- Only investigators use spells. Spells work against all monsters. Effective only in caster's space. Can benefit
  another investigator.
- Cast: successful Knowledge roll. Failing → can't cast that turn. Cast/failed spells face down; at next turn start
  all face up.
- Cast any time; sanity cost paid before effect. No sanity lost if Knowledge roll fails.

## Items
- Magical (marked) or ordinary. Magical weapons are magical attacks; no Knowledge roll needed. Unmarked = physical.
- 1H/2H: no more than two 1H or one 2H in any one attack. Some items need no hands.
- Item effective only within a space.
- After surviving a location event, any item worth more than $1 may be sold at half list price (round up) at Hib's
  Roadhouse or Curiositie Shoppe. $1 items can't be sold there. Discard item.
- Auction card drawn in place of an item still counts as the gained item.

## Charity
- At Dagon Mission with a charity card. Must have (1) no spells / no money / no items, or (2) fewest
  spells / money / items of all investigators. Charity granted in one category of player's choice that qualifies.
- Repayment: investigator must dispatch a monster or close a gate before the charity card can be used again; the
  Order collects all such trophies anywhere (back to monster pile / gate deck). Charity card usable any number of
  times. Can't discard to qualify. Flip to "Repay Charity With Good Works" while owed.

## Trades
- Money, charity, item, spell, local character cards tradable any time when traders occupy the same space.
  Skill cards only before the game begins.

## New investigator
- Selected or random. Starts at Train Station with 1 skill, 1 spell, 1 charity, 3 items, $13; 10 points STR/SAN, min 3.
- Begins action in the game turn following the demise of the previous one.
- New players may join at the start of any turn (victory conditions follow investigator count).

## Roll of Honor
- Most gates destroyed; tie → highest monster points (sum of SP of trophy monsters); still tied → record all.
- Record player, gates eliminated, trophy monster SP. OPTION: use accumulated gate SP to decide First Citizen.

## Q&A rulings
- Bind Monster on a gate: released monster sucked through gate with guardians; gate and all monsters to trophies.
  Bind vs another monster: bound monster released after battle, placed in any adjacent space, pointed any direction.
- Powers: destroying the gate sucks the power through → trophy.
- Only one gate per location ever; location event calling for second gate → only the monster.
- A location ride: roll on the new location table unless a monster is met or gate transit made.
- Powers and ghosts appear and remain in locations; only Tindalos can enter a location (only buildings).

## Terms
- Auction: drawer calls an auction, draws next two item cards (any additional auction cards drawn are discarded
  back into the deck). Bids for one item at a time starting at $1. Auctioneer may bid. Sold: auctioneer gets half
  the price, other half (incl. odd dollar) to bank. Unsold: auctioneer may pay list price. Auctioneer buying at bid
  pays full price. Bidders anywhere. Items may be bid at cash value (change from bank; auctioneer always receives cash).
- Attacks You in Buildings Only (Hound of Tindalos): cannot attack at Black Cave, Devil's Beach, Founder's Rock,
  Graveyard, Lake Miskatonic, Woods, the Abyss, Earth's Dreamlands, Plateau of Leng — and goes back into the …
  monster pile without effect (no replacement drawn). Once a Hound has inflicted damage → back to monster pile.
- Back Doors: Dagon Mission, Boarding House, Miskatonic University have two entrances. All entrances marked.
- Bind Monster: target must be on the gameboard; investigator in/moves to the target's space; successful Knowledge
  roll → (1) banish monster to trophy pile, or (2) monster moves with investigator and makes an attack against a
  monster or a gate in this or a later turn. Investigator adds Fight, spells, items to the bound monster's attack.
  Five powers immune. Bound monster doesn't count against strength; moves with investigator; ignore handedness.
  Bound attack = weapon; physical unless against same species (then magical).
  Success vs gate → bound monster + gate to trophies. Success vs monster → target to trophies, bound monster freed
  into any adjacent space of choice. Card usable once successfully, then returned to the spells deck.
- Blockers: halt vehicular movement one space distant — there must be an intervening space between blocker and traveler.
- Byakhee: flyers despite missing <flyer> on the front.
- Cloud Memory: +2 sanity to you or investigator of choice in same space/location.
- Discard: put card face up at the bottom of the deck; when a face-up card appears, reshuffle.
- Doom Factor: beyond 13 → game lost.
- Dread Curse of Azathoth: +9 magical attack, costs 2 sanity per successful cast.
- Drops You Through Nearest Gate (Nightgaunt): successful attack does no STR harm (check sanity) but carries the
  investigator through the nearest gate → Box 2 of that Other World; nightgaunt stays in the space. Grabbed in an
  Other World → back to Arkham through the gate by which you left.
- Elder Sign: go through any gate, return to Arkham, spend 2 sanity; gate card to trophies; Elder Sign card on
  location; doom marker back one. Not movable. Prevents further gates/monsters there. Doesn't stop Tindalos entering.
- Fast Talk: no use vs monsters. Successful Fast Talk during a purchase buys item/translation for $1 less than list.
  OPTION: failing raises cost by $1.
- Fight: built-in physical attack; never vs monster immune to physical. OPTION: two investigators in the same space
  who both attack monsters each receive +1 Fight bonus.
- Find Gate: only in an Other World. Return to Arkham after arriving — before movement, or before combat with a
  monster after surviving the sanity roll.
- Flesh Ward: automatically cancels any one monster attack or counterattack in a turn.
- Flyers: F-n; no handedness; move toward nearest investigator on the sidewalk (street). Except Tindalos, flyers
  do not enter locations after appearing.
- Ghosts: like powers, don't move; any number can stay in a location. Sneak past ghosts and powers (to visit OW or
  close gate) → sanity roll against each guardian. To attack one guardian, sanity roll only for that one.
- Guardians: normally a power or ghost.
- Handed: circle/diamond → right or left per R/L notation.
- Harmed By Magic And Silver Bullet (Werewolf): harmed by magical items, spells, and the silver bullet fired from any gun.
- Heal: successful cast adds 2 STR to specified investigator in same space.
- Killed, Put in Monster Pile; Take 1 Item (Migo): counter returns to pile; no trophy; draw 1 Item card.
- Knowledge: roll each time a spell is cast.
- Local Characters: +2 Fight each. May substitute for the investigator by taking one attack or counterattack from a
  monster (Arkham or OW), then discarded. Not items; don't carry/use items.
- Magical attacks: spells, magical items, a monster attacking another of its own species.
- Mists of R'lyeh: one automatically successful Sneak per turn.
- Monsters: 49 pieces.
- Only Magic Harms It: only spells/magical items; Fight and ordinary items useless; D6 still applies. Same species
  bound counts as magical.
- Origination classes: 9 colour schemes; 8 match Other Worlds; 9th black-and-white = unaffected by gate closing.
- Physical attack: Fight, ordinary weapons, D6 roll, Mythos monster attacks.
- Powder of Ibn-Ghazi: +7 magical, 1 SAN per cast. Power Drain: +6 magical, 1 SAN per cast.
- Powers: do not move; immune to Bind Monster. Multiple powers/ghosts can stack on a location.
- Retainers: $2 per turn, found at Miskatonic U. and the Newspaper. Only four retainer cards exist …
  no more than four retainer payments are made per turn.
- Shrivelling: +6 magical, 1 SAN per cast.
- Sneak: success → any adjacent space or location, then roll sanity (most ghastly only). Fail → must confront.
  Not usable if all adjacent spaces/locations contain monsters; not vs Hound of Tindalos in a building.
- Spaces: streets divided into spaces; locations and Other World boxes also "spaces".
- Taxis: stopping on one offers movement anywhere for $1.
- Tindalos: only monster that can enter a location from the street; only buildings. Non-buildings: Black Cave,
  Devil's Beach, Founder's Rock, Graveyard, Lake Miskatonic, Woods. In Other Worlds: no encounter in Abyss,
  Earth's Dreamlands, Plateau of Leng. In non-buildings the Hound moves through investigators and vice versa with no
  effect. Back to pile after any attack that inflicts STR loss. Cannot be Sneaked against.
- Trophy stacks: monsters and gates eliminated. Most gates (tiebreak gates+monsters) → Roll of Honor.
- Unharmed By Guns: shotguns, rifles, pistols, revolvers (incl. SMG/derringer) no effect; everything else normal.
- Vampire: each Random Monster Appearance step (i.e. each Mythos gate step) +1 SP (token). Bound vampire attacks at
  present strength.
- Weapons Do 1 Point Damage (Shoggoth etc.): each physical attack component counts as 1 (Fight skill, each weapon,
  D6 counts? Example: Fight + revolver + sword = 3 points + D6). → each physical source contributes 1; D6 added normally.
- Blocking traffic diagram: blocker prevents vehicular movement in its own space AND all adjacent spaces (gray).
  Foot travellers move through all but the blocker's own space; may enter it but must stop.

## Errata (01 wellcome…pdf)
- Skill cards: shuffle and deal (backs differ in shade).
- Money: 32 × $1 and 10 × $10 (bank is effectively unlimited in the app).
- Counters: murky purple + black (Migo, Dhole) → Yuggoth. Lighter purple + white (Byakhee, Star Spawn) → Great Hall
  of Celaeno. A second purple Star Spawn matches no world → play it as City of the Great Race (or any consistent scheme).
- R'lyeh Star Spawn: turns right at intersections, moves 4.
- One Shoggoth's front shows a second black circle ("indigestion") — ignore.
- RULES: if all gates are on the board or in trophy stacks, ignore a result that calls for a new gate and monster;
  do not advance doom for it (16 gate cards).
- If all monsters are on the board or in trophy stacks, ignore a result calling for a monster.
- Origination classes: black-and-white monsters in a gated location when that gate is destroyed are removed from
  play and placed in the destroyer's trophy stack (i.e. guardians).
- PnP version fixes movement on the back of 2 fast Deep Ones.
